The problem. Analysts spend hours searching 300-page annual reports for specific facts such as revenue growth, headcount and risk disclosures. Keyword search misses paraphrases. A general chatbot answers confidently but often gets numbers wrong and cites nothing.

The goal. Ask a question in plain English and get an answer grounded only in the report, with the page it came from. If the report does not contain the answer, the system says so.

How we will know it works. We measure it on a hand-written set of 30-50 questions: retrieval hit rate, faithfulness (is the answer supported by the retrieved text?), latency, and cost per query. We report the numbers we actually measure.
